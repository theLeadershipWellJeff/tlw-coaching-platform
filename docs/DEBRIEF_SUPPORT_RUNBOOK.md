# Assessment Debrief — support runbook

**For:** Caleb (first-line support for portal participants).
**Where you work:** Business Center → Command Center → **Assessment Debrief**
→ **Support** tab, plus the **Portal users** tab. You need a supervisor
sign-in; Jeff grants it.

**Tone.** Warm, short, plain. These are leaders who just received feedback
about themselves. Never comment on anyone's scores, never speculate about who
rated them, never promise a change to their report.

---

## How tickets arrive

A participant without a coach sees **Contact support** in their portal. Sending
it creates a ticket in the Support tab and emails the support inbox. Reply from
the Support tab, not from your own email, so the thread is recorded.

## The eight things people will ask

### 1. "I didn't get my sign-in link"
1. Portal users tab → find them → check "Portal" column. "Not invited" means
   nobody sent one yet; "invited <date>" means it went out.
2. Ask them to check spam and search for **theLeadershipWell**.
3. Click **Resend**. Limit: 5 links per hour per person. If it says too many,
   wait an hour.
4. If their email is wrong on our side, **Edit** → fix email → Resend.

### 2. "The link says it's expired / already used"
Links work once and expire after 24 hours. Click **Resend**. Tell them to use
the newest email.

### 3. "I'm locked out" (password)
Eight wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. Tell them to wait 15
minutes, or click **Resend** so they can get in with a link and set a new
password from Settings inside the portal. You cannot see or reset their
password. Nobody can.

### 4. "I can't see my report"
Portal users tab → "Report" column.
- **none**: the report hasn't been uploaded. Tell Jeff which participant.
- **failed** with "name mismatch": the name on the PDF doesn't match their
  record. Documents tab → find it → check the name → **Confirm it's theirs**
  if it clearly is (e.g. "Bob" vs "Robert"). If it isn't, tell Jeff.
- **failed** (anything else) or **unsupported**: don't retry more than once.
  Screenshot the error, tell Jeff. The file stays downloadable for the
  participant meanwhile.
- **complete** but "360: off": the flag is off. Click it to turn on.

### 5. "The assistant said something wrong about my scores"
Ask for a screenshot. Open their PDF (Documents tab → the participant's
report) and check the number. If the assistant misquoted a number, that's a
bug: send Jeff the screenshot and the report. If the participant is reading
the band differently from the score, explain gently that bands come from
percentile norms, not the raw score, and that a higher score can sit in a
lower band. That's how the instrument works.

### 6. "Who gave me that comment / score?"
We don't know, and the system doesn't know. Rater names are removed before
the report is read. Say so plainly and kindly. Do not guess. Do not look.

### 7. "Can you delete my data?"
Yes. Tell Jeff the participant's name and email. Jeff removes the documents
and conversations. Confirm to the participant by email when done. Never
promise a timeline shorter than five working days.

### 8. "I want to talk to a real coach"
Send them the booking link (the same one behind **Talk to a coach** in their
portal) or, for cohort participants, the debrief coach's name from the
Companies & cohorts tab. Jeff decides anything beyond that.

## Never

- Never send a report PDF by email. It's in their portal, always.
- Never turn off download. There is no such setting, on purpose.
- Never speculate about raters, scores, or what a participant "should" do.
- Never share one participant's anything with another, or with their company.
- Never paste anything from a report into a ticket reply.

## Escalate to Jeff when

- A report fails or is unsupported after one retry.
- A participant disputes a number the assistant quoted.
- Anyone asks about deletion, legal, HR, or their employer seeing data.
- Anything you're unsure about. Close the loop with the participant either
  way: "I've passed this to Jeff, you'll hear back by <day>."

## Closing tickets

Reply first, then **Close ticket**. If they write again, it reopens itself in
spirit — click **Reopen**, reply, close again.
